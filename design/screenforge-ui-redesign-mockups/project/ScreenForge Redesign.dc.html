2Edge pill: docked to the right edge of the screen, always within reach

A floating panel that doesn't take focus (the frontmost window stays the capture target), docked to the right edge of the screen and draggable vertically. At rest it's a 14 px tab (logo + agent status). On hover it opens: capture the frontmost window, pick a window, paste, open the canvas; at the bottom, the agent status and the number of instructions. After a capture, a card lets you write the instruction right away without opening the app. Try it: hover the pill, click the teal button.

Direction
Taken from your references: a pro-tool shell (floating toolbar and panels, dense rows) with a prismatic teal → indigo → magenta identity from the logo and the neon street. Color has two jobs: teal for tools and selection, magenta only for anything the agent reads. Geist + Geist Mono.
Key changes
Icon toolbar with shortcut letters on each button; Rect / Ellipse / Polygon and Line / Arrow are grouped (8 buttons instead of 11). Instructions come right after Name, in their own block, and show as numbered magenta pins on the canvas (hover a pin for a preview). The agent's state sits in the title bar. Zoom + Fit all at bottom left. Errors appear as toasts on the canvas.
Interactive
The mockups work: click elements or layers, type in the instructions (pins update live), use V F R O L A X P T C and Esc after clicking the canvas, zoom, open the flyouts. Below 1200 px, Layers turns into a floating panel (1d). Export is the main action in the title bar; its label follows the selection.
